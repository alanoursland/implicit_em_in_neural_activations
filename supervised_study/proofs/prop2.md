# Proposition 2 -- Composition Forfeits the Uniform Bound

Verification of `theory.tex`, Proposition `prop:composition` (the paper's title claim). Generated by `proofs/prop2.py`.

> The NLS-Jacobian norm was derived incorrectly by hand several times (claimed `<= 2`, then `1+sqrt(K)` with a wrong tightness/range claim) before being computed. The correct closed form is `||J||_2 = sqrt(K)*||r||_2 <= sqrt(K)`.

- **Label clamp drops from the Hessian**: CE-logit Hessian equals `diag(p) - pp^T` for every label c. **PASS**
- **Kernel**: `J @ 1 = 0` for every simplex r (ones vector in kernel; this is why the triangle bound `1+sqrt(K)` is loose). **PASS**
- **Closed-form spectrum**: eigenvalues of `J^T J` are exactly `{K*s (once), 0 (once), 1 (K-2 times)}` with `s = ||r||_2^2`, so `||J||_2 = sqrt(K)*||r||_2 <= sqrt(K)` for ALL simplex r (not only vertices). Verified vs numeric spectrum over K in {4,6,10,25,64}; worst eigenvalue mismatch `4.62e-14`. **PASS**
- **Spectrum (symbolic spot-check)**: at exact simplex points (uniform and two-point), the top eigenvalue of `J^T J` equals `K*s`. **PASS**
- **Gauss-Newton (leading) term** `||J^T W2^T (diag p - pp^T) W2 J||_2 <= (K/2) sigma_max(W2)^2`: holds over random W2, p, r; worst LHS/bound ratio `0.194` (<= 1). **PASS**
- **Residual is real (scope check)**: the NLS second-order term is present and NON-negligible -- the full Hessian differs from the Gauss-Newton term (worst residual/full norm ratio `1.00`). The proposition therefore bounds only the leading term, not the full Hessian. **PASS (residual confirmed present)**

The leading factor `(K/2) sigma_max(W2)^2` depends on the **learned** spectrum of the corridor W2 (and on K), unlike Lemma 1's fixed data constant. That dependence -- introduced by composition and absent from the isolated site -- is the forfeited guarantee. The full composed curvature (including the residual) is measured empirically in the paper.

**Overall: ALL CHECKS PASS.**
